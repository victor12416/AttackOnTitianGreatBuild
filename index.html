District footprint (fan estimate)

Footprint, wall thickness, and gate width are editable estimates. Thickness grows outward, so it does not shrink the protected radius.

Evidence & limits

Approximate anime scale: Wall Maria radius is commonly read as about 480 km; the official portal publishes the series information cards, while the map is explicitly broad-scale. [1][2]

Shiganshina: southern Wall Maria outcrop; the district outer gate and Wall Maria inner gate are different structures. [3][4]

Footprint: 8.87 km wide × 11.467 km deep is a fan-derived estimate from schematic art, not a canon survey. Streets and building footprints remain unconfirmed until source frames are calibrated. [5]
